# Roadmap

The 2.0 redesign is the current product. Near-term work stays deliberately small:

- Verify every quotation against a named translation or edition and record the result in `data/texts.json`.
- Correct content or citations when better primary-source evidence is found, without inventing certainty.
- Keep keyboard, reduced-motion, responsive, performance, and content-integrity checks green as the site changes.
- Remove `legacy/` after one release if no migration issue requires it.

There are no committed plans for VR, AR, an AI guide, community accounts, or a PWA. New product scope requires a separate decision rather than living indefinitely in this list.
